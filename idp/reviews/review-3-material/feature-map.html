KinSync Feature Map: what's built, what's next

KinSync quietly learns an elderly person's normal phone rhythm on their own phone, and alerts family when that rhythm goes silent. Raw activity data never leaves the phone. This map shows every planned feature against the IDP review schedule.

The privacy promise

  • Raw data (unlocks, app names, motion, calls) stays on the phone.
  • Only an "I'm OK" heartbeat and the expected check-in window are sent to the server.
  • The server has no table that could hold raw activity.
  • No GPS location, ever.

Out of scope for version 1

  • Fall detection and medical diagnosis
  • Step counting and continuous GPS tracking
  • Wearables, iOS, audio or video monitoring
  • Multiple languages; more than 3 caregivers per elder
